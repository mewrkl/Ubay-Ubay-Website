Good to know

Making more than 4 designs

You can make as many designs as you want, but only 4 designs go into one bundle.

  • 4 designs make 1 bundle.
  • 5 designs still make 1 bundle. The extra design does not earn more.
  • 8 designs make 2 bundles and a bigger cut.
Solo pins
  • Only artists who submitted a bundle can send solo pin designs.
  • Solo pin art does not have to be new or on theme.
  • Solo pins go in their own folder, named like this: LastName_solo pins_ArtistName

The backcard

You only design the pins and the bundle name. An assigned Ubay-Ubay artist makes the backcard so all bundles look the same.

From top to bottom: your bundle name, "UP Cookout 2026", your 4 pins with the Ubay-Ubay logo in the center, and your social handle. The card is vellum board or hard paper with pre-punched holes for the pins.

Want something added to the card? Message @anachimotte on Instagram.
