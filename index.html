Frequently asked questions

When is the deadline?

The last day of every month. Upload your designs with your upload link by then. The month after, the team prints and packs your bundle. See "How to join" below.

Can I make more than 4 designs?

Yes. Make as many as you want, but only 4 designs go into one bundle.

  • 4 designs make 1 bundle.
  • 5 designs still make 1 bundle. The extra design does not earn more.
  • 8 designs make 2 bundles and a bigger cut.
Can I also send single pins?

Yes, as solo pins, if you also submitted a bundle.

  • Solo pin art does not have to be new or on theme.
  • Upload solo pins with the same upload link as your bundle.
Who designs the backcard?

A FASO artist makes every backcard, so all bundles look the same. You only design the pins and the bundle name logo. See the sample card under "Design 4 pins".

The card is vellum board or hard paper with pre-punched holes for the pins.

Want something added to your card? Message @anachimotte on Instagram.
