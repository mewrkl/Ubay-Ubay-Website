Ubay-Ubay is a pop-up art market by Tinkers, an organization in the Fine Arts department of UP Cebu. We hold pop-up markets in school to raise funds and to give student and local artists a place to sell their work. This year, we are bringing a stall to UP Cookout 2026, one of UP Cebu's biggest annual events, on November 14 and 15.

We are inviting you to design a button pin bundle for our stall. You make the art. We print, pack, and sell it, and you earn a cut of every sale.

4. Frequently asked questions

When is the deadline?

October 20 to 25, 2026. [final date to be announced] Upload your designs to the submission folder by then. See "How to join" below.

Can I make more than 4 designs?

Yes. Make as many as you want, but only 4 designs go into one bundle.

  • 4 designs make 1 bundle.
  • 5 designs still make 1 bundle. The extra design does not earn more.
  • 8 designs make 2 bundles and a bigger cut.
Can I also send single pins?

Yes, as solo pins, if you also submitted a bundle.

  • Solo pin art does not have to be new or on theme.
  • Solo pins go in their own folder, named like this: LastName_solo pins_ArtistName

Solo pin submission folder [link to be added]

Who designs the backcard?

An assigned Ubay-Ubay artist makes every backcard, so all bundles look the same. You only design the pins and the bundle name. See the sample card under number 1.

The card is vellum board or hard paper with pre-punched holes for the pins.

Want something added to your card? Message @anachimotte on Instagram.
